About GhostChat

Your conversations. Your payments. One place.

Bring Matrix messaging, wallet-linked usernames, MST payments and a P2P test trading flow into one messenger. GhostChat is being built for people and communities who want to talk, send payments and coordinate exchanges without leaving their conversation workspace.

Use test coins only. GhostChat is experimental and unaudited. Payment addresses, amounts and blockchain activity are public.

The problem we are working on

Conversations, wallet addresses and payment updates often live in different apps. People have to switch between them to agree on a payment, identify the recipient and check what happened. GhostChat brings those steps together while keeping wallet approvals explicit.

Three parts of one messenger

01

Matrix conversations

Connect a Matrix account for messaging and configure end-to-end encryption for supported chats. A working homeserver is required for delivery.

02

MST payments

Look up a registered @username or enter a full wallet address. Review the destination, then send testnet tokens, share a payment link or fund community allocations.

03

P2P test trading

Lock native faucet tMSTC in escrow, try a mock INR payment through Razorpay Test Mode and approve release after server verification.

What you can try today

  • BridgeKey wallet sign-in and a unique payment username.
  • One workspace for conversations, testnet payments and P2P tools.
  • A native tMSTC escrow with Razorpay Test Mode payment verification.

Live Matrix messaging needs a configured homeserver and cross-device verification. Test Checkout requires configured Razorpay credentials. The P2P demo uses no real INR or mainnet MSTC. Its server attestation is not a zero-knowledge proof, and a funded public end-to-end run remains to be verified.

Where we are going
