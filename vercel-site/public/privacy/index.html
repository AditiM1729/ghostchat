Privacy notice

How this testnet MVP handles information.

Updated October 29, 2026. Operator: Prem Bharne, an independent developer.

Public blockchain and directory information

Wallet addresses, transaction amounts, contract state and transaction history are public on the blockchain. Signed claim requests and approvals are readable through the relay API. Registered usernames and wallet bindings are publicly searchable. Avoid putting personal or confidential information in campaign titles or public records.

Browser storage

The app stores preview messages, preferences and local payment history on your device. Matrix session information uses browser session storage; its SDK also uses local browser storage for messaging and encryption state. BridgeKey stores and manages wallet signing keys; GhostChat stores its login session only in memory. Public app assets may be stored by the service worker for offline use. Clearing browser storage can remove local chat data and encryption keys; keep your chat recovery backup.

Hosted services and providers

The hosted relay stores claim requests, signatures, username registrations and rate-limit records. Its rate limiter hashes the connecting IP address into time-window keys. Hosting, RPC, wallet and messaging providers may separately process IP addresses, device information and request metadata under their policies.

Providers used by the application include Vercel, Cloudflare, Reown and public blockchain RPC services. Price lookups use CoinGecko or Coinbase. Connecting to a Matrix homeserver shares account and messaging metadata with that server. End-to-end encryption does not make all metadata private; the local messenger preview is neither delivered nor end-to-end encrypted.

Retention and requests

Blockchain records cannot be deleted by GhostChat. The username service currently treats registrations as permanent and does not offer a self-service rename or deletion flow. Retention periods for hosted claim records and operational logs are not yet formalized. Contact the founder about access or deletion requests; some public, contractual or technical records may not be removable. This MVP has no self-service data export or deletion dashboard.
