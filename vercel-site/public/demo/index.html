Product walkthrough

From your first conversation to a test payment.

This is an explanatory walkthrough of the account, messaging and payment flow. Changing steps does not connect an account, submit a transaction or create a payment. The linked app screens let you explore the actual tools.

STEP 1 OF 5 · You

Create your account

Connect BridgeKey, sign a wallet ownership message, then choose your name, mobile number and unique @username.

Your username links to your wallet for payment lookup. Your mobile number is private and collecting it does not verify ownership. Keep your BridgeKey recovery phrase private.

Before using the testnet app

Use different seller and buyer wallets with faucet tMSTC for deposits and gas. Confirm the intended recipient’s full wallet address. Keep the submitted transaction hash and trade link; the P2P demo saves public pending-transaction references on this device for recovery.

What this walkthrough does not promise

No live INR trading, sponsored gas, guaranteed delivery, private blockchain payments, ZK payment verification or independent security audit is provided. Local preview messages stay in your browser; delivered chats need a connected Matrix account and homeserver.
