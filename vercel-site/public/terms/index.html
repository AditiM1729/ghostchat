Testnet demo terms

Conditions for exploring the experimental demo.

Updated October 29, 2026. Provided by Prem Bharne as a pre-incorporation project.

Testnet use only

Use supported testnets and test coins. Do not use GhostChat to send real money, make real INR/UPI payments or hold assets of value. The current P2P demo uses native faucet tMSTC and mock INR through Razorpay Test Mode. The legacy sandbox uses freely minted gpUSD. GhostChat is not offering banking, investment, exchange or guaranteed settlement services.

Wallet control and transactions

You control your wallet and are responsible for reviewing the network, contract, destination, amount and wallet prompts. Never share your seed phrase or private key. Transactions consume network gas and confirmed transfers may be irreversible. The P2P test server can sign payment attestations after verification; it does not hold your wallet key or submit the buyer’s release transaction.

Expiry and recovery

Recovery depends on the selected flow. Unclaimed payment links and community funds support their contract-specific expiry refunds. An accepted P2P trade cannot be reclaimed by the seller on timeout; the buyer can intentionally return its allocation. Test funds may remain locked if payment evidence or buyer cooperation is unavailable. Browser history is not a wallet backup or a complete payment index. Save transaction hashes, links and your encrypted wallet backup.

Availability and limitations

The demo is experimental, unaudited and provided without a service-level or recovery guarantee. Providers may fail or rate-limit requests. Live messaging, physical-device behavior and funded flows on additional testnets need further verification. Features, infrastructure and these notices may change as the project develops.

Appropriate use and contact

Do not impersonate others, abuse shared infrastructure or submit confidential information to public blockchain or directory records. Questions and problems can be reported to prembhrane455@gmail.com. These demo conditions do not assert any regulatory approval or independent security certification.
